# PBI-00012: Read claims in bcr extract

## Directive

Make `bcr extract` read the `claims` of each breadcrumb and print them
as `claim` records, as `specs/extract/spec.md` describes: single-quoted
entries of `TARGET KIND ARGUMENT`, one kind, `has-line`, and a claim
with a problem dropped on its own (ADR-0017, ADR-0018, ADR-0019).

**Scope:**
- In `internal/frontmatter`, allow a single-quoted value only as an
  entry of `claims`, and report an entry of `claims` that is plain, in
  double quotes, or has a line comment after it. Every other value
  keeps today's rules.
- In `internal/crumb`, add the claim: its target, kind and argument,
  with its line. Check each entry's three parts, its target, and its
  kind and argument against the kinds the core knows, which today is
  `has-line` alone. A problem in a claim drops that claim only; a
  problem in `id`, `type` or `links` still drops the whole breadcrumb.
  `claims` is optional, and a bare `claims:` is a problem.
- In `internal/records`, write one `claim` record for each valid claim,
  after the breadcrumb's links. `records.Read` keeps ignoring `claim`
  records, so `bcr verify` does not change.
- Exit 1 when a claim was dropped, while still printing the rest of
  its breadcrumb.
- Add a test for each Scenario of `specs/extract/spec.md` that has
  none, and tick its Definition of Done.
- Document the `claim` record and the problems of a claim entry in
  `docs/bcr.md` under `### extract`, and generate `docs/bcr.1` again.
- Do not check a claim against its target: that is `bcr audit`'s, in
  slice 2c.
- Leave `docs/breadcrumb-sdd.md`, `bcr check` and `bcr verdict` as
  they are: their `contains` claim belongs to the old model.

## Refinement Protocol

to the spec in the same commit. If it needs a change to the record,
to what is a problem, or to the exit status the spec defines, stop
and ask.
