# PBI-00035: Adopt VISION.md, and bring the project's principles and heuristics into the repository

## Directive

Add `VISION.md`, following ASDLC's Product Vision pattern, and list it
in `specs/asdlc/spec.md`.

**Scope:**
- Write `VISION.md`: who Breadcrumb is for, its point of view and six
  principles, and five decision heuristics, each with a case.
- In `specs/asdlc/spec.md`, add Product Vision to the patterns table,
  say where `VISION.md` lives and that it decides nothing, and claim
  its `## Decision heuristics` line.
- Outside the spec, needing no PBI of their own (ADR-0028), in the
  same change: design heuristics in `ARCHITECTURE.md`; rules of thumb,
  the ADR-or-spec test and the "and" rule for ADR titles in
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`; `VISION.md` in `AGENTS.md`'s Context Map and a
  check before naming a Go module; the stability promise before 1.0
  and a pointer to `VISION.md` in `README.md`.
