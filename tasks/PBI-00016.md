# PBI-00016: Judge a claim and a breadcrumb in the core

## Directive

Add to `internal/crumb` the rules `bcr audit` needs, as
`specs/audit/spec.md` describes: whether a claim holds in the content
of its target, and the verdict of a breadcrumb from its claims and its
problems (ADR-0019, ADR-0022). Write `specs/audit/spec.md`.

**Scope:**
- Add `specs/audit/spec.md`, whole, with its Definition of Done not
  yet ticked and no claim: the command comes with PBI-00018.
- Add the verdict: Confirmed, Refuted or Undecided.
- Keep the rule of a kind of claim in one place: the check of its
  argument, which `bcr extract` uses, and whether it holds, which
  `bcr audit` will use. `has-line` holds when a line of the content,
  without its line ending and without the spaces and tabs at its
  ends, is equal to the text, byte for byte.
- A claim whose target is not a file is Refuted. The core is told
  whether it is one, and is given its content; it opens no file.
- Make the check of a claim's target, kind and argument usable on its
  own, so `bcr audit` can refuse a `claim` record `bcr extract` could
  not print. The problems `bcr extract` prints do not change.
- Add the verdict of a breadcrumb: Refuted when a claim is Refuted,
  otherwise Undecided when it has a problem, otherwise Confirmed when
  it has a claim; with no claim and no problem, Undecided with the
  reason `no claims`.
- Do not read records or files, and do not add the command: those are
  PBI-00017 and PBI-00018.

## Refinement Protocol

the same commit. If it needs a change to what `has-line` checks, or to
how a breadcrumb's verdict is built, stop and ask: those are ADR-0019
and ADR-0022.
