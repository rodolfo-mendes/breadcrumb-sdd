# PBI-00005: Remove bcr list

## Directive

Remove the command `bcr list`, which `bcr extract` replaces, as the
Definition of Done of `specs/extract/spec.md` asks.

**Scope:**
- Remove `runList`, `listUsage` and the `list` case from
  `cmd/bcr/main.go`, and `list` from its package comment.
- Remove the tests of `bcr list` from `cmd/bcr/main_test.go`, and
  `list` from its table of usage errors.
- Remove `bcr list` from the SYNOPSIS of `docs/bcr.md`, and its
  `### list` subsection. Change the example under `### links` that
  pipes into `bcr list -` so that it no longer uses it. Generate
  `docs/bcr.1` again (`go test ./internal/manpage -update`).
- Leave `breadcrumbs/` as it is. TK-0012's claims quote `listUsage`
  and `### list`, so TK-0012 turns Refuted, and with it RQ-0027,
  RQ-0028, RQ-0029, RQ-0030, TD-0026 and IN-0011. Their claims are not
  edited (`AGENTS.md`).
- Leave `bcr links`, `bcr verdict` and the other commands as they are.
