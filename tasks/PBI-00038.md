# PBI-00038: Show a breadcrumb's title in the report's diagrams

## Directive

Let a breadcrumb carry a `title`, as ADR-0032 decides, and show it in
the boxes of the diagrams `bcr report` draws, under the id. Until now
a box showed an id, a type and a verdict, and a reader opened the file
to learn what `ADR-0019` is about.

**Scope:**
- In `internal/crumb`, read `title` under `breadcrumb`: optional, one
  line of text, not empty, with no tab. A title that breaks this is a
  problem that leaves no breadcrumb.
- In `internal/records`, print the title as `TITLE`, a sixth field at
  the end of the `breadcrumb` record, left out when there is none.
  Only `bcr report` reads it.
- In `internal/page`, write the title on its own line of the box's
  label, after the id. A breadcrumb with no title keeps the label it
  had.
- Describe both in `specs/extract/spec.md` and `specs/report/spec.md`,
  with a test for each new Scenario, and write
  `internal/page/testdata/small.golden.md` again.
- Document the key, the field and the label in `docs/bcr.md`, and
  generate `docs/bcr.1` again.
- Leave the breadcrumbs of this repository as they are: giving each a
  title is its own change.
- Leave the files `bcr init --layout` writes as they are.
- Leave `specs/verify/spec.md`, `specs/audit/spec.md` and
  `specs/filter/spec.md` as they are: they already read no field
  after the fifth of a `breadcrumb` record.
