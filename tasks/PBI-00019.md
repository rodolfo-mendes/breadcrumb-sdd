# PBI-00019: Build the views of bcr report from records

## Directive

Give `bcr report` what it draws, as `specs/report/spec.md` describes:
every record the pipe prints, read into one set, and the view of each
spec. Put the rule that says what is in a view in the core (ADR-0007,
ADR-0013).

**Scope:**
- Add `specs/report/spec.md`, with its breadcrumb. Its claim on
  `docs/bcr.md` comes with the command, in PBI-00020.
- In `internal/crumb`, let a set hold the type of a breadcrumb, the
  verb of a link, and the verdicts given before. Give the view of each
  spec: the spec, what links to it, what it links to, and the links
  among those. Give the order by id, the claims of each breadcrumb,
  and the verdict given to each claim and breadcrumb, or that none
  was.
- In `internal/records`, add a reader for `bcr report`: `breadcrumb`,
  `link`, `claim`, `problem`, `claim-verdict` and `verdict` records,
  with unknown kinds skipped. A verdict record whose `VERDICT` is not
  one of the three is not a record.
- Leave the readers `bcr verify` and `bcr audit` use as they are: they
  still skip the kinds they do not read, and fill no type and no verb.
- Do not add the page or the command: that is PBI-00020.
