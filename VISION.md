# Breadcrumb-SDD - Vision
----

The premise of the Breadcrumb-SDD method is that programming languages still are the best tools for detailed
software-specification. Thus, neither Markdwon files nor other forms of structured natural language are replacements
for code. 

The specifications introduced by our method do not replace code nor intend to. Code, or the final product of the repository
remains being the ultimate source of truth of a repository. In our method, specifications are called Breadcrumbs: an artifact that sits
on the audit chain, carries the upward link back to what it came from, and exists only because a judgment gate fired to put it there.
Or in other words: a set of human-readable, linked artifacts that keep agents on rails and make deviation visible fast.

Having said that, the goals of this repository are:
- Provide the formal specification of Breadcrumb-SDD method
- Provide tools and executables that setup the Breadcrumb-SDD method in a repository

Moreover, the Breadcrum-SDD method is self-contained: it applies to itself to develop.
