# RQ-0011: getopt-style flags

Parent: [IN-0007](IN-0007.md)

A `bcr` command reads its flags in getopt style:

- a short flag is `-` and one letter; short flags that take no value
  can be combined, so `-ab` is `-a -b`;
- a short flag's value follows it, attached or as the next argument:
  `-oFILE` or `-o FILE`;
- a long flag is `--` and a name; its value follows `=` or comes as
  the next argument: `--output=FILE` or `--output FILE`;
- `--` ends the flags; every argument after it is an operand.

> flags follow getopt style, with short flags that can be combined,
> such as `-o`, beside long ones
