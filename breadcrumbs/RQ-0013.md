# RQ-0013: The contract documents every command

Parent: [IN-0006](IN-0006.md)

The contract of `bcr` documents each of its commands: how it is run,
its flags, what it reads, what it writes and in what format, and its
exit codes.

> Write a document that specifies the interface of the `bcr` toolkit:
> its commands, flags, inputs, outputs, exit codes and output formats.
