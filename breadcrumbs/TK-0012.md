`bcr list` prints each breadcrumb's id, type and title, and
`bcr links` each link from a breadcrumb to its parent, with `-r`
following the links up. `bcr verdict`, `bcr list` and `bcr links` take
their operands through one function, `selected`.

TK-0010's claims on `bcr verdict` still hold: the lines they quote
moved into `selected` unchanged.
