# TD-0019: An Intake may derive from another Intake

## Decision

An Intake may name another Intake as its parent (TD-0005), when it
asks for one part of what the parent asks for. It keeps the parent's
words where it can, and adds only what the asker decided since.

## Why

IN-0005 asks for more than one change can carry out. Split into
smaller Intakes, each part can be built and checked on its own, and
the parent still records the ask as it was made.

TD-0003 records an ask in its asker's words but does not say what to
do with an ask too large for one change.

## What it beat

- Splitting the ask straight into Requirements: IN-0005 leaves open
  what to build, such as the commands, and a Requirement cannot leave
  that open.
- Editing IN-0005 into a smaller ask: its original words would be
  gone from the tree.
