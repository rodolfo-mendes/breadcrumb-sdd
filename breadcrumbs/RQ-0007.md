# RQ-0007: A Task's verdict comes from its claims

Parent: [IN-0004](IN-0004.md)

A Task is Confirmed when every one of its claims holds against the
repository's files, and Refuted when any claim fails or cannot be
read. A Task with no claims is Undecided.

> A Task is Confirmed when all its claims hold, and Refuted when any of
> them fails or cannot be read.
