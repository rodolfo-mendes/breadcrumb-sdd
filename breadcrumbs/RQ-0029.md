# RQ-0029: bcr list and bcr links of the breadcrumbs they are given

Parent: [IN-0011](IN-0011.md)

`bcr list` and `bcr links` take operands as `bcr verdict` does
(RQ-0025): ids, paths, or `-` for standard input. Given operands,
`bcr list` prints only those breadcrumbs, and `bcr links` only the
links they have, in the order given.

Both exit with 0, since they judge nothing; with 2 when an operand
names no breadcrumb, still printing the others.

> With the verdicts, they let a pipe follow the chain from a Refuted
> Task up to what it breaks.
