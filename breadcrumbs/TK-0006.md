`bcr` reads its flags with a getopt-style parser in `internal/cli`,
which also has the reader of breadcrumbs from standard input. No
command takes breadcrumbs yet, so RQ-0012 is not a parent. `bcr audit-report`
writes results to standard output and messages, starting with `bcr: `,
to standard error, and exits 0, 1 or 2 as RQ-0010 defines.

TD-0018 and TD-0019 shape the breadcrumbs, not the code; the
breadcrumbs they allow are IN-0005 to IN-0012, and this Task's own
Parent lines.
