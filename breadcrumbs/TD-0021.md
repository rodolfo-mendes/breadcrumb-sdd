# TD-0021: A getopt-style flag parser of our own

Parent: [IN-0007](IN-0007.md)

## Decision

`bcr` reads its flags with a small parser in its own package,
`internal/cli`, that follows RQ-0011. Each command declares its flags,
short and long, and whether each takes a value.

## Why

Go's `flag` package cannot combine short flags, and it treats `-name`
and `--name` alike. A library would break TD-0010. A parser for the
subset RQ-0011 asks for is short, and can be tested on its own.

## What it beat

- Go's `flag` package: in the standard library, but not getopt style.
- A library such as `pflag`: getopt style, but a dependency
  (TD-0010).
- Parsing flags inside each command: every command would repeat it.
