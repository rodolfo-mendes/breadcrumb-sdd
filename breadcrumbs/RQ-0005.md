# RQ-0005: Nodes are colored by verdict

Parent: [IN-0001](IN-0001.md)

The report colors a rectangle green when its breadcrumb is Confirmed,
red when it is Refuted, and gray when it is Undecided.

> And one of the most important pieces of information: the color!
> Green nodes are Confirmed. Red nodes are Refuted. Gray nodes are
> Undecided.
