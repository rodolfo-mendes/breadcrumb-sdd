# TD-0003: Record asks as Intakes

## Decision

An Intake records an ask for this repository, in the words of whoever
made it. Each lives in `breadcrumbs/` in a file named `IN-NNNN.md`,
where `NNNN` is a four-digit number.

An Intake may be edited after it is committed, to fix its wording or
to follow a changed ask. Git keeps its earlier words.

## Why

An ask rewritten into what the software must do loses its gaps, and
the gaps are what later explain a drift. Kept in its asker's words,
the ask can still be read against what was built from it.

## What it beat

- Writing Requirements straight from the ask: shorter, but the
  original words are gone once they are sharpened.
- Keeping the ask in `README.md`: the README describes the repository
  as it is, and changes with it.
