# TD-0024: The model finds the problems, with their lines

Parent: [IN-0009](IN-0009.md)

## Decision

A problem is found by the model in `internal/breadcrumb`, and carries
the number of the line where it is, counted from 1. `bcr check` and
`bcr audit-report` both show the problems the model finds, and find
none of their own.

## Why

A rule about what can be read belongs to the model (TD-0009). Found
in one place, the two commands cannot disagree on what a problem is.

## What it beat

- A separate checker in `bcr check`: two readers of one format,
  drifting apart.
- Problems without lines, and `bcr check` searching the file for the
  text: a text can appear on more than one line.
