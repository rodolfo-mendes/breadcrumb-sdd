# RQ-0032: What a new breadcrumb holds

Parent: [IN-0012](IN-0012.md)

A breadcrumb made by `bcr new` starts with its title line,
`# ID: TITLE`, followed by one `Parent:` line for each parent, in the
order given (TD-0005). A Technical Decision also gets empty
`## Decision`, `## Why` and `## What it beat` sections, and a Task an
empty `## Claims` section. `bcr check` finds no problem in it.

> Numbering and the `Parent:` lines are easy to get wrong by hand.
