# TD-0032: Turn the specification into HTML with our own converter

Parent: [IN-0016](IN-0016.md)

## Decision

`cmd/specsite` turns the specification into HTML with a converter of
its own, in `internal/specsite`, written with the standard library.
It reads the part of Markdown TD-0022 allows for the contract, and
nothing more. The specification keeps to that part: its table of
types becomes a list, a change of wording that keeps its version
(TD-0030).

## Why

The specification uses few elements of Markdown, as the contract
does, and `internal/manpage` shows a converter for them is short and
testable. Kept to the same part of Markdown, the specification can
also become a man page, and reads the same as plain text from
`bcr spec`.

## What it beat

- Jekyll, which GitHub Pages runs on Markdown: no code, but a Ruby
  tool this repository neither controls nor tests, and its own rules
  for links and ids.
- A Go Markdown library such as goldmark: complete, but a dependency
  (TD-0010) for a handful of elements.
- Tables in the converter: one table in the specification, for a
  reader and a writer that the contract does not need.
