# RQ-0030: bcr links follows the chain up

Parent: [IN-0011](IN-0011.md)

`bcr links -r`, or `--recursive`, also prints the links of each
parent it reaches, and of their parents, up to the breadcrumbs with
none. Each link is printed once, the first time it is reached.

> they let a pipe follow the chain from a Refuted Task up to what it
> breaks
