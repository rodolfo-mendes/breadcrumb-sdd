# TD-0014: License the repository under MIT

## Decision

Everything in this repository, code and prose, is licensed under the
MIT License, in `LICENSE` at the repository root.

## Why

The method only works if teams adopt it, so the license should put
nothing in the way of use, including in closed or commercial work.
MIT is the shortest license that does this, and almost no legal team
rejects it.

## What it beat

- Apache-2.0: grants patent rights explicitly and covers incoming
  contributions, but at ten times the length. With one author, the
  patent grant protects against no one yet.
- MPL-2.0 and AGPL-3.0: they keep changes open, and they make the
  tool harder to adopt.
- Separate licenses for code and prose: two licenses to explain, for
  no gain yet.
