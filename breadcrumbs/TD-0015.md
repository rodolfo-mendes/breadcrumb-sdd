# TD-0015: How contributions come in

## Decision

`CONTRIBUTING.md` at the repository root guides contributors, people
and agents. It summarizes the breadcrumbs; where the two disagree, the
breadcrumbs win.

A contribution is licensed under the repository's license, MIT
(TD-0014), with no separate agreement or sign-off.

A contributor who is not a maintainer opens an issue before proposing
an Intake or a Requirement, since a maintainer approves each one
(TD-0006).

## Why

A contributor should not have to read every breadcrumb to make a first
change. The guide gives the order of the work; the breadcrumbs keep
the rules.

MIT says nothing about contributions coming in, so the guide says it.

## What it beat

- Rules written in the guide itself: two sources of one rule, drifting
  apart.
- A Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off on every commit: explicit,
  but one more step for every contributor, for a risk that is small
  while the repository has few of them.
