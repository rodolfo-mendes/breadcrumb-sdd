# IN-0012: Author breadcrumbs with bcr

Parent: [IN-0005](IN-0005.md)

Add a `bcr new` command that creates a breadcrumb of a given type,
with its title and parents, under the next free number, and prints the
path of the new file.

Motivation:

- The toolkit's scope grows to cover an interface for authoring
  breadcrumbs, for both users and agents.
- Numbering and the `Parent:` lines are easy to get wrong by hand.
