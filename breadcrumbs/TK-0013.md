`bcr new TYPE TITLE [PARENT|-]...` creates a breadcrumb under the next
free number of its type, with its title, its `Parent:` lines and, for
a Technical Decision or a Task, empty sections, and prints its path.
It writes nothing when it cannot write it right, and never replaces a
file. This Task was created with `bcr new`.
